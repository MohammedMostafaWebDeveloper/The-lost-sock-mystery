A guy once had two pairs of socks, one of them was red and the other was white
One day, he woke up to get to work, when he was about to leave, he found out that one white sock disappeared. He wore the red one anyways.
But the next day something worse happened, one red sock disappeared too. He had no choice but to wear a white sock and a red sock. When he went to work, everyone notices it and it was awkward.
Since then, he decided to found and organization to search in lost socks cases, they managed to find some but others we lost forever.
